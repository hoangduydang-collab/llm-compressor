Goal 02 / engineering field note · Work in progress

The temporary evaluation pipeline

The reproducible, fail-closed paired harness is built and has produced its four-way comparison against BF16. A fifth in-house-AWQ arm and the migration onto the team's official pipeline are in progress.

4 checkpoints compared lm-eval-harness 0.4.12 · vLLM Paired fidelity, not leaderboard scores

01 / PurposeComparable model-to-model evidence

Every checkpoint receives the same rendered prompts, exact examples, generation seeds, decoding settings, and scoring implementation.

The completed deliverable is a working paired evaluation pipeline with fail-closed contract checks and reproducible raw evidence. It provides a controlled quantization-fidelity signal for internal decisions.

It is deliberately not a public-leaderboard score: the evaluation uses seeded subsets and a fixed internal contract rather than each benchmark's full public protocol.

What the result supports

Our in-house GPTQ W4A8 remains within a few points of BF16 on every task. Official MXFP8 is roughly baseline-equivalent and is the strongest quant overall. The external AWQ comparator loses materially more quality, especially on GPQA and AIME25.

03 / HarnessReproducible setup

  • Framework: EleutherAI lm-evaluation-harness 0.4.12, direct vLLM backend.
  • Models: in-house GPTQ W4A8, in-house AWQ W4AFP8 (r5, smoke-gated), cyankiwi AWQ W4A16, official MXFP8 W8A16, and official BF16.
  • Tasks: GPQA-Diamond 100, MMLU-Pro 100, GSM8K-CoT 100, and the full 30-question AIME25 split.
  • Generation: seeds 42/1234/4158, thinking enabled, temperature 1.0, top-p 0.95, maximum 16,384 generated tokens.
  • Serving: TP8 for quantized and MXFP8 checkpoints; BF16 uses TP16 across two nodes.

The preflight contract pins the sample manifest, tokenizer, chat template, rendered prompts, and evaluation config. Cross-run gates require those inputs to match before scores are compared.

04 / NextA fifth arm, then the official pipeline

The harness itself is built and appropriate for paired internal decisions — though its confidence intervals are wider than full benchmark runs, and the numbers should not be quoted as leaderboard results. Two things remain in progress.

A fifth arm. The in-house full-calibration AWQ (r5, W4AFP8 — Goal 3's artifact) cleared the official smoke gate on 2026-07-20: contract-gated against the BF16 r50 root, every scoring-relevant hash matched, and the single eval_config_sha256 mismatch is the post-r50 NCCL PG-timeout knob (b56dacf6, documented in the run root's COMPARABILITY.md). Its production-profile scores are pending.

From stopgap to the official pipeline

The team's official evaluation pipeline now exists as its own repository (AICloud/benchmarks) — lm-eval 0.4.10 [api] run against a self-deployed OpenAI-compatible endpoint, plus distribution, reliability, and agentic layers this stopgap lacks. It uses different tasks, greedy decoding, and loglikelihood MC scoring, so its scores are not comparable to §02. Migrating MiniMax-M3 onto it — serve on a pinned patched vLLM 0.24.0 image, wire the four comparator profiles — is the active work; this harness holds only until M3 runs there.
