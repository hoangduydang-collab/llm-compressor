Goal 02 / engineering field note · Work in progress

The temporary evaluation pipeline

A paired harness for one practical decision: how closely do quantized checkpoints track BF16 when prompts, samples, decoding, and scoring are held constant?

4 checkpoints compared lm-eval-harness 0.4.12 · vLLM Paired fidelity, not leaderboard scores

01 / PurposeComparable model-to-model evidence

Every checkpoint receives the same rendered prompts, exact examples, generation seeds, decoding settings, and scoring implementation.

The result is a controlled quantization-fidelity signal for internal decisions. It is deliberately not a public-leaderboard score: the evaluation uses seeded subsets and a fixed internal contract rather than each benchmark's full public protocol.

What the result supports

Our in-house GPTQ W4A8 remains within a few points of BF16 on every task. Official MXFP8 is roughly baseline-equivalent and is the strongest quant overall. The external AWQ comparator loses materially more quality, especially on GPQA and AIME25.

03 / HarnessReproducible setup

  • Framework: EleutherAI lm-evaluation-harness 0.4.12, direct vLLM backend.
  • Models: in-house GPTQ W4A8, cyankiwi AWQ W4A16, official MXFP8 W8A16, and official BF16.
  • Tasks: GPQA-Diamond 100, MMLU-Pro 100, GSM8K-CoT 100, and the full 30-question AIME25 split.
  • Generation: seeds 42/1234/4158, thinking enabled, temperature 1.0, top-p 0.95, maximum 16,384 generated tokens.
  • Serving: TP8 for quantized and MXFP8 checkpoints; BF16 uses TP16 across two nodes.

The preflight contract pins the sample manifest, tokenizer, chat template, rendered prompts, and evaluation config. Cross-run gates require those inputs to match before scores are compared.

04 / NextMove from harness proof to routine evaluation

These subsets are appropriate for paired internal decisions, but their confidence intervals are wider than full benchmark runs and the numbers should not be quoted as leaderboard results.

  1. Generate the merged four-way report with BF16-relative flip rates and paired significance statistics.
  2. Apply this ready quality-evaluation pipeline to the next real full-calibration checkpoint after its static and serving gates pass.
